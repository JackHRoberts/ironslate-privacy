IronSlate Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

IronSlate is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as important, what it doesn't.

Your workout data

All information you enter — workouts, exercises, routines, goals, and history — is stored only in a local database on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere, and the app has no user accounts or sign-up. We (the developer) have no way to see, access, or recover this data. It is never included in the crash reports described below.

Crash and error reporting

IronSlate uses Sentry, a third-party error-monitoring service, to learn about crashes and bugs. When the app encounters a fatal error, Sentry automatically receives technical diagnostic information: the error message and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, and a short technical history of app events (such as screen navigation or network status changes) leading up to the crash. This is used solely to identify and fix bugs. See Sentry's privacy policy for how they handle this data. IronSlate does not use analytics, advertising, or any other tracking software.

Data sharing

Your workout data is never shared with anyone, because it never leaves your device. Crash diagnostics (described above) are shared only with Sentry, and only for the purpose of debugging.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device. Uninstalling IronSlate permanently removes your local database along with it.

Children's privacy

IronSlate does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Your workout data stays on your device, and crash reports contain only technical diagnostic information, never anything you've entered into the app.

Changes to this policy

If IronSlate's data practices change further, this policy will be updated first and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
