IronSlate Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

IronSlate is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as important, what it doesn't.

Data collection

IronSlate does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, and does not connect to the internet or any server. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

Data storage

All information you enter — workouts, exercises, routines, goals, and history — is stored only in a local database on your device. It is never sent anywhere. We (the developer) have no way to see, access, or recover this data.

Data sharing

Because no data ever leaves your device, none is shared with third parties, advertisers, or anyone else.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by deleting the app from your device. Uninstalling IronSlate permanently removes your local database along with it.

Children's privacy

IronSlate does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If IronSlate's data practices change in the future (for example, if crash reporting is added), this policy will be updated first and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact
